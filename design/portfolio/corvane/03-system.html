Tone of voice
01

Measured.

We say less and mean all of it. One idea to a sentence; no exclamation marks.

02

Precise.

Numbers before adjectives. Dates, figures and names, never “soon” or “significant”.

03

Courteous.

Warm, never familiar. Titles and surnames until a client invites otherwise.

We say
“Your quarterly review is ready. Zara Malik will call on Thursday at 11:00 to take you through it.”
Not
Great news! Your exclusive wealth update has just landed.
