The client card

Cut from brass, engraved, never printed.

The private client card is brushed brass, 0.8 mm thick. Symbol, name and horizon line are engraved and left unfilled, so the card wears in rather than out. Family office principals carry it in ink-green PVD, the engraving cut back to bright metal.

Material
Brushed brass, linear satin grain
Engraving
0.08 mm, unfilled
Tiers
Private client · Family office
