Brand identity · Concept02 / 07 — Symbol
The symbol

A raven, drawn
with a compass.

Corvane takes its name from corvus, the raven, and the vane that reads the wind: the keeper and the instrument. The symbol holds both. Three circles and two horizontals resolve into a single initial C; look again and it is a raven in profile, its beak resting exactly on the horizon.

Every curve is struck from a fixed centre and every straight edge is level, so the mark engraves, foils and renders at sixteen pixels without redrawing.
